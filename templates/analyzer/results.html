How is this score calculated?

Required requirements carry a weight of 2; preferred requirements carry a weight of 1. Supported earns full credit, partially supported earns half, and not demonstrated or unclear earns zero. Coverage = earned credit ÷ total possible credit × 100, rounded to the nearest whole number. Requirements and their interpretation are generated by AI and can be incomplete or mistaken. Quotes are checked against the submitted text; a quote alone does not prove qualification.
